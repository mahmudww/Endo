# Story

In a distant place lies an island nation called Endo. It was once a green, beautiful land, rich in natural resources.

But one day, the ruler of Endo fell ill and died. Power was then handed over to someone named Dororo.

At first, Dororo ruled Endo wisely.

But slowly, everything began to change.

Natural resources were exploited everywhere. Forests were burned, rivers turned black from industrial waste, and the air became increasingly polluted. Massive factories were built at the cost of forests that were supposed to serve as the lungs of the nation.

The air became so polluted that everyone was eventually required to wear masks.

Clean water became expensive. Owning a home became increasingly difficult for lower-income communities. Some people were even forced to live in makeshift buildings in the corners of the cities.

Farmers and fishermen gradually lost their livelihoods as their land and seas became polluted, making it harder and harder to survive.

The people began to lose their way.

As life became more difficult, anger became easier to provoke. Some chose to flatter and follow the ruler, believing that serving those in power was the easiest way to survive.

The government distributed free food to the people.

But... the food was not entirely safe.

Rebellions happened again and again.

And again and again, they failed to overthrow Dororo.

Until, during the final rebellion, the people discovered a much more terrifying truth:

The government of Endo was no longer run by humans.

Behind the government stood the Devil, an entity from another dimension.

Those who opposed the government began forming groups, gathering people and resources little by little, waiting for one opportunity to fight back.

That day has finally come.

A group of citizens has launched an attack on the Endo government office, where the nation's highest officials work.

And among them is you.

An Ordinary Citizen who wants change.

# Gameplay

To save Endo, you must face five figures standing between the people and those in power.

### 1. THE FOLLOWER

A human who chose to become a loyal follower and flatterer of the Endo government.

### 2. THE GATEKEEPER

The guardian of the Endo government office, who turns out to be something other than human.

### 3. LILBA, THE MIDDLE DEVIL

A middle-ranking Devil who has gained a position within the Endo government.

### 4. TED, THE HIGH DEVIL

A high-ranking Devil who stands beside Dororo and protects his rule.

### 5. DORORO, THE TYRANNY KING

The tyrant who has forced countless people in Endo to live in suffering.

But you will not fight them with swords or weapons.

You will fight them through music.

The Devils use cursed songs to manipulate humanity. To defeat them, you must break their rhythm and turn the music against them.

Every beat becomes an opportunity to attack.

**PERFECT**, **EXCELLENT**, and **GOOD** deal different amounts of damage, while **MISS** or pressing the wrong lane causes you to take damage.

On PC or laptop, follow the falling notes using the **Q**, **W**, and **E** keys on your keyboard.

On mobile, tap the corresponding button when the note reaches its target.

Read the rhythm. Do not lose the beat.

Because in Endo...

> **Every beat can become an act of resistance.**
